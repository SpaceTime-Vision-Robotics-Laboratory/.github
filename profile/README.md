# SpaceTime Vision and Robotics Laboratory

</div>

We are a research team from **National University of Science and Technology Politehnica of Bucharest** working on Computer Vision, Robotics, High Performance Computing, and Autonomous Drones.

## What We Do

- Unsupervised learning in spatiotemporal data
- Scene understanding and vision-to-language
- Autonomous drone navigation and visual servoing
- Smart cameras for real-world applications
